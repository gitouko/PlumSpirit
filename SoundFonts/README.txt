PlumSpirit automatically checks `SoundFonts/GeneralUser-GS.sf2` beside its EXE as a fallback. If you use a custom SoundFont, that setting or a project-specific SoundFont may take priority. The SoundFont may be changed through PlumSpirit's audio settings.
